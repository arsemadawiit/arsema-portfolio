01 — Overview

The Project

We developed Tandem, a learner-centered EdTech platform that shifts children's online safety away from surveillance and restriction toward active family communication, digital literacy, and shared reflection.

The Problem

Traditional online safety tools focus heavily on blocking, screen-time tracking, or isolated school lessons. This treats safety primarily as a monitoring problem rather than an ongoing learning process and leaves parents under-supported.

The Solution

Tandem positions the parent as the primary learner, providing contextual tools that help families notice activity, interpret behavior, participate in conversations, and plan appropriate follow-up.

02 — Research

Understanding the Problem

We explored how parents currently navigate their children's technology use and where existing online-safety approaches leave gaps in understanding and communication.

01

Parent Interviews

Conducted semi-structured interviews with parents of children aged 7–13, exploring current technology use, unsupervised online time, safety strategies, and communication gaps.

02

Existing Mediation Approaches

Analyzed limitations in existing mediation approaches and identified a gap in scaffolded, just-in-time learning that helps parents interpret risks rather than immediately defaulting to blocking applications.

03 — Synthesis

From Monitoring to Guidance

The research reframed online safety as more than a question of how much parents can monitor. Tandem instead focuses on helping parents understand what they see and decide how to respond constructively.

01

Context before restriction.

Rather than immediately reducing children's access, parents need support interpreting digital activity and potential risks.

02

Parents need learning support too.

Designing for online safety means supporting parents as learners instead of assuming they already know how to interpret every digital behavior.

03

Safety can become a conversation.

The experience should encourage active family communication and shared reflection rather than relying solely on passive surveillance.

04 — Design

Designing Tandem

Tandem translates these ideas into a guided experience that moves parents from noticing digital activity to interpreting it and deciding how they want to respond.

Feature 01

Family-Visible Activity Dashboard

The dashboard creates a shared starting point for understanding digital activity, giving parents visibility into patterns that may require additional context or conversation.

Feature 02

Refined AI Interpretations

Instead of presenting activity without explanation, Tandem uses contextual AI guidance to help parents consider plausible interpretations before deciding what action to take.

Feature 03

Platform Context & Parent Guidance

Platform-specific context helps parents understand the environment around an activity, including opportunities, safety tools, and their role. This supports constructive participation without exposing private message content or defaulting to surveillance.

05 — Outcome

The Final Experience

Together, Tandem's features create a continuous flow from noticing patterns to interpreting behavior, participating in family conversations, and planning what comes next.

06 — Reflection

Key Takeaways

01

Scaffolding Parent Confidence

Designing for adult learners requires tools that prompt collaborative family conversations rather than relying solely on monitoring or blocking.

02

Formative Feedback Loops

Continuous cycles of noticing patterns, interpreting behavior, planning responses, and evaluating outcomes can support longer-term learning.

03

Cross-Functional Collaboration

I gained experience coordinating research findings, learning-theory frameworks, and rapid UI prototyping across a multidisciplinary team.
